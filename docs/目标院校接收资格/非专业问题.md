**English:**

Thank you for the question. I'd handle it in three steps.

Third, if it's genuinely a bad fit, discuss adjusting the entry point with my advisor—start with a smaller sub-problem, get some initial results, build confidence, then gradually tackle harder parts. ## 六、如果回答不出来应该怎么办

**1. 英文没听清 / 没听懂**
- 礼貌请求重复: "Sorry, could you please repeat the question?"
- 请求换种说法: "Sorry, I didn't quite catch that. Could you rephrase it, please?"
- 确认理解: "Do you mean ... ?" (用自己的话复述一遍, 确认是否理解正确)
- 注意: 请求重复一两次是完全可以接受的, 但不要反复要求, 否则会显得听力或准备不足. 

**2. 中文问题没听懂 / 不理解题意**
- 诚恳请求澄清: "老师, 不好意思, 您能再解释一下这个问题的意思吗?"
- 复述确认: "您是想问……这个方面吗?"
- 不要不懂装懂, 强行作答, 答偏了反而扣分. 

**3. 确实不会回答**
- 先诚实承认: "这个问题我目前了解得还不够深入. "
- 再说出自己知道的相关部分: "不过据我所知……" (展示思考过程和相关知识)
- 表达学习意愿: "这方面我了解得还比较有限, 但我很愿意在之后深入学习. "
- 尝试从相关角度切入: 即使不能直接回答, 也可以说说自己的思路, 联想到的知识点, 展现思维过程. 
- 切忌: 长时间沉默, 胡编乱造, 不懂装懂. 

**4. 通用原则**
- 保持冷静, 不要慌张, 面试官更看重你的态度和思维, 而非全知全能. 
- 诚实 + 积极 + 展示思路, 比强行编造答案更好. 
- 面试结束后可以主动查漏补缺, 把没答上来的问题弄懂. 

**English:**

It's completely normal to get stuck during an interview. What matters is being honest and handling it gracefully—don't make things up or stay silent for too long.

**1. If you didn't hear or understand the English question**
- Ask politely for repetition: "Sorry, could you please repeat the question?"
- Ask for rephrasing: "Sorry, I didn't quite catch that. Could you rephrase it, please?"
- Confirm your understanding: "Do you mean ... ?" (paraphrase it back to check)
- Note: Asking once or twice is fine, but don't keep asking repeatedly—it may suggest weak listening or preparation.

**2. If you don't understand a Chinese question**
- Ask sincerely for clarification: "老师, 不好意思, 您能再解释一下这个问题的意思吗?"
- Paraphrase to confirm: "您是想问……这个方面吗?"
- Don't pretend to understand and answer blindly—a wrong answer costs more than asking.

**3. If you genuinely don't know the answer**
- Admit it honestly first: "这个问题我目前了解得还不够深入. "
- Then share what you do know: "不过据我所知……" (show your thinking and related knowledge)
- Express willingness to learn: "这方面我了解得还比较有限, 但我很愿意在之后深入学习. "
- Try approaching from a related angle: even if you can't answer directly, share your reasoning and related points to show how you think.
- Avoid: long silence, making things up, pretending to know.

**4. General principles**
- Stay calm. Interviewers value your attitude and thinking more than omniscience.
- Honesty + positivity + showing your reasoning beats fabricating an answer.
- After the interview, review what you missed and fill the gaps.
