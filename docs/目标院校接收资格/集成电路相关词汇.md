# 集成电路相关词汇

## 一, 半导体物理与器件(Semiconductor Physics and Devices)

1. **semiconductor** 半导体

- 拓展短语: intrinsic semiconductor 本征半导体, compound semiconductor 化合物半导体, organic semiconductor 有机半导体
- 例句: Silicon is the most widely used semiconductor material in the modern microelectronics industry.

2. **band gap** 带隙; 禁带宽度

- 拓展短语: energy band gap 能量带隙, narrow band gap 窄带隙, wide band gap 宽禁带
- 例句: The band gap of a semiconductor determines its optical and electrical properties.

3. **carrier** 载流子

- 拓展短语: majority carrier 多数载流子, minority carrier 少数载流子, charge carrier 电荷载流子
- 例句: Both electrons and holes act as charge carriers in semiconductor materials.

4. **doping** 掺杂

- 拓展短语: ion implantation doping 离子注入掺杂, in-situ doping 原位掺杂, heavy doping 重掺杂
- 例句: Doping is a key process to adjust the conductivity of semiconductor wafers.

5. **mobility** 迁移率

- 拓展短语: electron mobility 电子迁移率, hole mobility 空穴迁移率, carrier mobility 载流子迁移率
- 例句: High carrier mobility is critical for achieving high-performance semiconductor devices.

6. **conductivity** 电导率

- 拓展短语: electrical conductivity 电导率, thermal conductivity 热导率, ionic conductivity 离子电导率
- 例句: The conductivity of a semiconductor increases with rising temperature within a certain range.

7. **PN junction** PN结

- 拓展短语: PN junction diode PN结二极管, forward-biased PN junction 正偏PN结, reverse-biased PN junction 反偏PN结
- 例句: A PN junction forms the basic structure of most semiconductor diodes and transistors.

8. **depletion region** 耗尽区

- 拓展短语: depletion layer 耗尽层, space charge region 空间电荷区
- 例句: The width of the depletion region changes with the applied bias voltage.

9. **Fermi level** 费米能级

- 拓展短语: Fermi energy 费米能量, quasi-Fermi level 准费米能级
- 例句: The Fermi level represents the highest occupied energy state of electrons at absolute zero.

10. **intrinsic** 本征的

- 拓展短语: intrinsic semiconductor 本征半导体, intrinsic carrier concentration 本征载流子浓度
- 例句: Intrinsic silicon has very low conductivity at room temperature.

11. **extrinsic** 非本征的; 杂质的

- 拓展短语: extrinsic semiconductor 杂质半导体, extrinsic doping 外源掺杂
- 例句: Extrinsic semiconductors are fabricated by introducing specific impurities into intrinsic materials.

12. **drift** 漂移

- 拓展短语: drift current 漂移电流, drift velocity 漂移速度
- 例句: Drift current is generated by the movement of charge carriers under an external electric field.

13. **diffusion** 扩散

- 拓展短语: diffusion current 扩散电流, diffusion coefficient 扩散系数
- 例句: Diffusion current arises from the concentration gradient of charge carriers in semiconductors.

14. **recombination** 复合

- 拓展短语: electron-hole recombination 电子空穴复合, radiative recombination 辐射复合, non-radiative recombination 非辐射复合
- 例句: Carrier recombination is the main cause of energy loss in light-emitting devices.

15. **generation** 载流子产生

- 拓展短语: carrier generation 载流子生成, thermal generation 热激发产生
- 例句: Optical excitation leads to the generation of electron-hole pairs in semiconductors.

16. **diode** 二极管

- 拓展短语: light-emitting diode 发光二极管, Zener diode 齐纳二极管, Schottky diode 肖特基二极管
- 例句: A diode allows electric current to flow in only one direction under forward bias.

17. **BJT (Bipolar Junction Transistor)** 双极结型晶体管

- 拓展短语: NPN BJT NPN型双极晶体管, PNP BJT PNP型双极晶体管, power BJT 功率双极晶体管
- 例句: BJT is a current-controlled semiconductor device widely used in analog circuits.

18. **MOSFET** 金属-氧化物-半导体场效应晶体管

- 拓展短语: NMOS N型金属氧化物半导体, PMOS P型金属氧化物半导体, power MOSFET 功率场效应管
- 例句: MOSFET is the fundamental building block of modern digital integrated circuits.

19. **channel** 沟道

- 拓展短语: inversion channel 反型层沟道, channel length 沟道长度, channel width 沟道宽度
- 例句: Short-channel effects become significant when the channel length of MOSFET scales down to nanometers.

20. **threshold voltage** 阈值电压

- 拓展短语: flat band voltage 平带电压, threshold voltage shift 阈值电压漂移
- 例句: The threshold voltage is the minimum gate voltage required to form an inversion channel in a MOSFET.

21. **short-channel effect** 短沟道效应

- 拓展短语: channel length modulation 沟道长度调制, drain-induced barrier lowering 漏致势垒降低
- 例句: Short-channel effects degrade device performance and cause increased power consumption.

22. **leakage current** 漏电流

- 拓展短语: subthreshold leakage current 亚阈值漏电流, gate leakage current 栅极漏电流
- 例句: Leakage current dominates static power consumption in advanced nanometer CMOS processes.

## 二, 集成电路设计(Integrated Circuit Design)

### (一)数字集成电路设计

1. **inverter** 反相器

- 拓展短语: CMOS inverter CMOS反相器, static inverter 静态反相器
- 例句: A CMOS inverter is the most basic logic gate in digital integrated circuit design.

2. **NAND gate** 与非门

- 拓展短语: two-input NAND gate 二输入与非门, CMOS NAND gate CMOS与非门
- 例句: NAND gates are universal gates that can be used to construct any other logic function.

3. **NOR gate** 或非门

- 拓展短语: NOR logic 或非逻辑, static NOR gate 静态或非门
- 例句: A NOR gate outputs a low signal when any of its inputs is high.

4. **flip-flop** 触发器

- 拓展短语: D flip-flop D型触发器, JK flip-flop JK触发器, clocked flip-flop 时钟触发器
- 例句: Flip-flops are the basic storage elements in sequential logic circuits.

5. **latch** 锁存器

- 拓展短语: SR latch SR锁存器, transparent latch 透明锁存器
- 例句: A latch is level-sensitive, while a flip-flop is edge-triggered.

6. **multiplexer (MUX)** 多路选择器; 数据选择器

- 拓展短语: 4-to-1 multiplexer 四选一多路选择器, analog multiplexer 模拟多路选择器
- 例句: A multiplexer selects one of several input signals and forwards it to a single output line.

7. **decoder** 译码器

- 拓展短语: 3-to-8 decoder 三八译码器, BCD decoder BCD译码器
- 例句: A decoder converts binary code into a one-hot output signal for control purposes.

8. **ALU (Arithmetic Logic Unit)** 算术逻辑单元

- 拓展短语: bit-slice ALU 位片式算术逻辑单元, floating-point ALU 浮点算术逻辑单元
- 例句: The ALU performs arithmetic and logical operations in a central processing unit.

9. **sequential circuit** 时序电路

- 拓展短语: synchronous sequential circuit 同步时序电路, asynchronous sequential circuit 异步时序电路
- 例句: Sequential circuits rely on clock signals to synchronize state transitions.

10. **combinational circuit** 组合电路

- 拓展短语: combinational logic 组合逻辑, combinational path 组合路径
- 例句: The output of a combinational circuit depends only on its current input values.

11. **clock** 时钟

- 拓展短语: clock frequency 时钟频率, clock skew 时钟偏移, clock jitter 时钟抖动
- 例句: High-speed digital circuits require stable clock signals with low jitter.

12. **register** 寄存器

- 拓展短语: shift register 移位寄存器, configuration register 配置寄存器
- 例句: Registers temporarily store data during the execution of processor instructions.

13. **counter** 计数器

- 拓展短语: binary counter 二进制计数器, ripple counter 纹波计数器, synchronous counter 同步计数器
- 例句: A counter can count the number of clock pulses and generate timing signals.

14. **FPGA (Field-Programmable Gate Array)** 现场可编程门阵列

- 拓展短语: FPGA prototyping FPGA原型验证, reconfigurable FPGA 可重构现场可编程门阵列
- 例句: FPGA is widely used for rapid prototyping of digital integrated circuits.

15. **ASIC (Application-Specific Integrated Circuit)** 专用集成电路

- 拓展短语: full-custom ASIC 全定制专用集成电路, semi-custom ASIC 半定制专用集成电路
- 例句: ASIC chips are designed for specific applications to achieve optimal performance and power efficiency.

16. **Verilog** Verilog硬件描述语言

- 拓展短语: Verilog HDL Verilog硬件描述语言, SystemVerilog 系统Verilog
- 例句: Engineers use Verilog to describe the structure and behavior of digital circuits at RTL level.

17. **VHDL** 超高速集成电路硬件描述语言

- 拓展短语: VHDL coding VHDL编程, behavioral VHDL 行为级VHDL
- 例句: VHDL is another widely used hardware description language for digital system design.

18. **RTL (Register Transfer Level)** 寄存器传输级

- 拓展短语: RTL design 寄存器传输级设计, RTL simulation 寄存器传输级仿真
- 例句: RTL description is the mainstream design abstraction level for digital ICs.

19. **power dissipation** 功耗

- 拓展短语: dynamic power dissipation 动态功耗, static power dissipation 静态功耗, leakage power 漏电流功耗
- 例句: Low power dissipation is a critical design goal for battery-powered electronic devices.

### (二)模拟集成电路设计

1. **op-amp (operational amplifier)** 运算放大器

- 拓展短语: differential op-amp 差分运算放大器, rail-to-rail op-amp 轨到轨运算放大器
- 例句: An ideal op-amp has infinite open-loop gain and zero input offset voltage.

2. **comparator** 比较器

- 拓展短语: high-speed comparator 高速比较器, hysteresis comparator 迟滞比较器
- 例句: A comparator compares two analog voltages and outputs a digital high or low signal.

3. **oscillator** 振荡器

- 拓展短语: ring oscillator 环形振荡器, crystal oscillator 晶体振荡器, LC oscillator 电感电容振荡器
- 例句: An oscillator generates a periodic analog signal without external input.

4. **PLL (Phase-Locked Loop)** 锁相环

- 拓展短语: charge-pump PLL 电荷泵锁相环, all-digital PLL 全数字锁相环
- 例句: PLL is widely used for clock generation and frequency synthesis in communication systems.

5. **ADC (Analog-to-Digital Converter)** 模数转换器

- 拓展短语: flash ADC 闪存型模数转换器, SAR ADC 逐次逼近型模数转换器, sigma-delta ADC 西格玛-德尔塔模数转换器
- 例句: An ADC converts continuous analog signals into discrete digital data.

6. **DAC (Digital-to-Analog Converter)** 数模转换器

- 拓展短语: current-steering DAC 电流舵数模转换器, resistor-string DAC 电阻串数模转换器
- 例句: A DAC converts digital binary data into corresponding analog voltage or current.

7. **filter** 滤波器

- 拓展短语: active filter 有源滤波器, passive filter 无源滤波器, low-pass filter 低通滤波器
- 例句: Analog filters are used to remove unwanted frequency components from signals.

8. **bandgap reference** 带隙基准

- 拓展短语: CMOS bandgap reference CMOS带隙基准源, high-precision bandgap reference 高精度带隙基准
- 例句: A bandgap reference provides a stable voltage reference independent of temperature and supply voltage.

9. **LDO (Low-Dropout Regulator)** 低压差线性稳压器

- 拓展短语: high-current LDO 大电流低压差稳压器, ultra-low noise LDO 超低噪声低压差稳压器
- 例句: LDO is widely used in portable electronics to provide clean power supply.

10. **gain** 增益

- 拓展短语: open-loop gain 开环增益, closed-loop gain 闭环增益, voltage gain 电压增益
- 例句: The open-loop gain of an operational amplifier typically exceeds 100,000.

### (三)集成电路版图与验证

1. **layout** 版图

- 拓展短语: full-custom layout 全定制版图, automatic layout 自动布局布线
- 例句: IC layout translates circuit schematics into physical geometric patterns on silicon.

2. **schematic** 原理图

- 拓展短语: circuit schematic 电路原理图, schematic capture 原理图输入
- 例句: Engineers first draw the circuit schematic before proceeding to physical layout design.

3. **DRC (Design Rule Check)** 设计规则检查

- 拓展短语: online DRC 在线设计规则检查, batch DRC 批量设计规则检查
- 例句: DRC verifies that the layout meets all manufacturing process design rules.

4. **LVS (Layout Versus Schematic)** 版图与原理图一致性检查

- 拓展短语: LVS verification 版图原理图一致性验证
- 例句: LVS ensures that the physical layout matches the original circuit schematic netlist.

5. **ERC (Electrical Rule Check)** 电气规则检查

- 拓展短语: ERC check 电气规则检查
- 例句: ERC detects electrical design errors such as floating nodes and short circuits.

6. **SPICE** 集成电路仿真程序

- 拓展短语: SPICE simulation SPICE仿真, HSPICE 高速SPICE仿真工具
- 例句: SPICE is the standard tool for analog and mixed-signal circuit simulation.

7. **netlist** 网表

- 拓展短语: gate-level netlist 门级网表, transistor-level netlist 晶体管级网表
- 例句: The netlist describes the connections between all components in a circuit.

8. **static timing analysis** 静态时序分析

- 拓展短语: STA 静态时序分析, timing closure 时序收敛
- 例句: Static timing analysis verifies that all timing constraints are met without dynamic simulation.

9. **setup time** 建立时间

- 拓展短语: setup time violation 建立时间违例
- 例句: Setup time is the minimum time data must be stable before the active clock edge.

10. **hold time** 保持时间

- 拓展短语: hold time violation 保持时间违例
- 例句: Hold time is the minimum time data must remain stable after the active clock edge.

## 三, 芯片制造工艺(Semiconductor Manufacturing Process)

1. **wafer** 晶圆; 硅片

- 拓展短语: silicon wafer 硅晶圆, 12-inch wafer 12英寸晶圆, wafer fabrication 晶圆制造
- 例句: High-purity single-crystal silicon wafers are the substrate for most integrated circuits.

2. **photolithography** 光刻

- 拓展短语: optical lithography 光学光刻, extreme ultraviolet lithography 极紫外光刻
- 例句: Photolithography transfers circuit patterns from a mask onto the wafer surface.

3. **photoresist** 光刻胶

- 拓展短语: positive photoresist 正性光刻胶, negative photoresist 负性光刻胶
- 例句: Photoresist is a light-sensitive material used to pattern wafers during lithography.

4. **mask** 掩模版

- 拓展短语: photomask 光掩模, reticle 掩膜版, mask set 掩模组
- 例句: Each mask layer defines a specific pattern for one manufacturing step.

5. **etching** 刻蚀

- 拓展短语: dry etching 干法刻蚀, wet etching 湿法刻蚀, reactive ion etching 反应离子刻蚀
- 例句: Etching removes unwanted material from the wafer surface according to the patterned photoresist.

6. **deposition** 沉积

- 拓展短语: thin film deposition 薄膜沉积, chemical deposition 化学沉积
- 例句: Deposition processes grow thin layers of dielectric, semiconductor, or metal materials on wafers.

7. **CVD (Chemical Vapor Deposition)** 化学气相沉积

- 拓展短语: PECVD 等离子体增强化学气相沉积, LPCVD 低压化学气相沉积
- 例句: CVD is widely used to deposit silicon dioxide and silicon nitride films in IC manufacturing.

8. **PVD (Physical Vapor Deposition)** 物理气相沉积

- 拓展短语: sputtering PVD 溅射物理气相沉积, evaporation PVD 蒸发物理气相沉积
- 例句: PVD is commonly used for depositing metal interconnect layers in semiconductor fabrication.

9. **oxidation** 氧化

- 拓展短语: thermal oxidation 热氧化, dry oxidation 干氧氧化, wet oxidation 湿氧氧化
- 例句: Thermal oxidation grows a high-quality silicon dioxide layer on the silicon wafer surface.

10. **ion implantation** 离子注入

- 拓展短语: high-energy ion implantation 高能离子注入, low-dose ion implantation 低剂量离子注入
- 例句: Ion implantation is the primary doping technique in modern semiconductor manufacturing.

11. **diffusion** 扩散(工艺)

- 拓展短语: thermal diffusion 热扩散, impurity diffusion 杂质扩散
- 例句: Diffusion was an early doping method, now largely replaced by ion implantation.

12. **CMP (Chemical Mechanical Planarization)** 化学机械平坦化

- 拓展短语: CMP slurry 化学机械抛光液, copper CMP 铜化学机械抛光
- 例句: CMP achieves global planarization of wafer surfaces for multi-layer interconnect fabrication.

13. **metallization** 金属化

- 拓展短语: copper metallization 铜金属化, aluminum metallization 铝金属化
- 例句: Metallization forms the conductive interconnects between transistors on a chip.

14. **planarization** 平坦化

- 拓展短语: global planarization 全局平坦化, local planarization 局部平坦化
- 例句: Planarization is essential for building multi-layer metal interconnects in advanced chips.

15. **cleanroom** 洁净室

- 拓展短语: Class 1 cleanroom 一级洁净室, cleanroom protocol 洁净室规范
- 例句: Semiconductor fabrication must be performed in ultra-clean cleanrooms to avoid particle contamination.

16. **yield** 良率

- 拓展短语: wafer yield 晶圆良率, die yield 芯片良率, yield improvement 良率提升
- 例句: Manufacturing yield is a key metric affecting the cost of semiconductor chips.

17. **defect** 缺陷

- 拓展短语: particle defect 颗粒缺陷, process defect 工艺缺陷, defect density 缺陷密度
- 例句: Even tiny defects on a wafer can cause functional failure of the final chip.

18. **interconnect** 互连线

- 拓展短语: metal interconnect 金属互连线, multi-level interconnect 多层互连线
- 例句: Advanced chips use up to 15 layers of copper interconnects for signal routing.

19. **via** 通孔; 过孔

- 拓展短语: contact via 接触孔, through-silicon via 硅通孔
- 例句: Vias connect different metal layers vertically in multi-layer interconnect structures.

20. **resolution** 分辨率

- 拓展短语: lithography resolution 光刻分辨率, resolution enhancement technology 分辨率增强技术
- 例句: The resolution of photolithography determines the minimum feature size that can be patterned.

21. **RET (Resolution Enhancement Technology)** 分辨率增强技术

- 拓展短语: optical proximity correction 光学邻近校正, phase shift mask 相移掩模
- 例句: RET extends the resolution limit of optical lithography for advanced technology nodes.

## 四, 微电子封装与测试(Microelectronic Packaging and Testing)

### (一)封装技术

1. **packaging** 封装

- 拓展短语: chip packaging 芯片封装, advanced packaging 先进封装, system-in-package 系统级封装
- 例句: Semiconductor packaging provides electrical connection, mechanical support, and protection for chips.

2. **encapsulation** 塑封; 包封

- 拓展短语: plastic encapsulation 塑料封装, epoxy encapsulation 环氧塑封
- 例句: Encapsulation protects the chip from physical damage and environmental moisture.

3. **wire bonding** 引线键合

- 拓展短语: gold wire bonding 金丝键合, copper wire bonding 铜丝键合
- 例句: Wire bonding is the most mature and widely used interconnection technology in packaging.

4. **flip chip** 倒装芯片

- 拓展短语: flip chip packaging 倒装芯片封装, flip chip bumping 倒装芯片凸点制备
- 例句: Flip chip technology offers higher I/O density and better electrical performance than wire bonding.

5. **BGA (Ball Grid Array)** 球栅阵列封装

- 拓展短语: PBGA 塑料球栅阵列, CBGA 陶瓷球栅阵列, flip chip BGA 倒装芯片球栅阵列
- 例句: BGA packages provide high pin count and excellent thermal performance for complex chips.

6. **QFP (Quad Flat Package)** 四方扁平封装

- 拓展短语: LQFP 薄型四方扁平封装, TQFP 薄型四方扁平封装
- 例句: QFP is a surface-mount package with leads extending from all four sides of the body.

7. **DIP (Dual In-line Package)** 双列直插封装

- 拓展短语: plastic DIP 塑料双列直插封装, ceramic DIP 陶瓷双列直插封装
- 例句: DIP was one of the earliest through-hole package types for integrated circuits.

8. **SiP (System-in-Package)** 系统级封装

- 拓展短语: heterogeneous SiP 异构系统级封装, 3D SiP 三维系统级封装
- 例句: SiP integrates multiple chips and passive components into a single package.

9. **lead frame** 引线框架

- 拓展短语: copper lead frame 铜引线框架, alloy lead frame 合金引线框架
- 例句: The lead frame provides electrical connections and mechanical support for the chip.

10. **substrate** 基板; 衬底

- 拓展短语: package substrate 封装基板, ceramic substrate 陶瓷基板, organic substrate 有机基板
- 例句: The package substrate routes electrical signals between the chip and the printed circuit board.

11. **underfill** 底部填充胶

- 拓展短语: underfill material 底部填充材料, capillary underfill 毛细管底部填充
- 例句: Underfill enhances the mechanical reliability of flip chip packages by reducing stress.

12. **thermal resistance** 热阻

- 拓展短语: junction-to-case thermal resistance 结壳热阻, junction-to-ambient thermal resistance 结环热阻
- 例句: Lower thermal resistance means better heat dissipation capability of the package.

13. **reliability** 可靠性

- 拓展短语: package reliability 封装可靠性, thermal cycling reliability 热循环可靠性

## 九, 各种从句翻译技巧(Translation Techniques for Clauses)

### (一)定语从句

1. **限制性定语从句**——通常译为前置定语, 用"的"字结构

- 原文: The architecture that we propose alleviates the memory-access bottleneck.
- 译文: 我们提出的架构缓解了访存瓶颈. 
- 技巧: 从句较短时, 直接译为"……的"前置修饰. 

2. **非限制性定语从句**——通常译为后置分句, 用"这""该""它"等衔接

- 原文: The design uses tiling, which improves data reuse significantly.
- 译文: 该设计采用了分块策略, 这显著提升了数据复用率. 
- 技巧: 用"这"承接整个主句, 单独成句更符合中文表达. 

3. **"介词+which/whom"结构**——译为状语或独立分句

- 原文: The platform on which the accelerator is implemented is Xilinx Zynq-7020.
- 译文: 该加速器所部署的平台是Xilinx Zynq-7020. 
- 技巧: 把介词短语还原为状语, 避免生硬直译. 

4. **as/which引导的非限制性定语从句**——译为"正如……""这一点……"

- 原文: As is shown in Table I, the throughput improves by 5.26x.
- 译文: 如表I所示, 吞吐率提升了5.26倍. 
- 技巧: "as is shown/depicted/illustrated"是论文高频套语, 固定译为"如……所示". 

5. **there be + 定语从句**——译为"有……的……"

- 原文: There are several factors that affect the timing closure.
- 译文: 有几个因素会影响时序收敛. 
- 技巧: 不必逐字译"有……的", 直接顺译更自然. 

### (二)状语从句

1. **时间状语从句**——译为"当……时""在……之后""随着……"

- 原文: When the channel length scales down, short-channel effects become significant.
- 译文: 当沟道长度缩小时, 短沟道效应变得显著. 
- 技巧: when不一定译"当", 也可译为"随着""如果". 

2. **原因状语从句**——译为"因为""由于""鉴于"

- 原文: Since the workload is memory-bound, reducing memory access is critical.
- 译文: 由于该工作负载受限于访存, 减少访存至关重要. 
- 技巧: since/because/as都可表原因, 按语气强弱选择. 

3. **结果状语从句**——译为"因此""从而""以至于"

- 原文: The tile size is too large, so that on-chip memory overflows.
- 译文: 分块尺寸过大, 以至于片上存储溢出. 
- 技巧: so that表结果时译为"以至于/从而", 表目的时译为"以便". 

4. **条件状语从句**——译为"如果""只要""除非"

- 原文: If the dataflow is properly designed, the DSP efficiency can be greatly improved.
- 译文: 如果数据流设计得当, DSP效率可以大幅提升. 
- 技巧: 条件句常可处理为"若……则……"的无主句结构. 

5. **让步状语从句**——译为"尽管""虽然""即使"

- 原文: Although the design achieves high throughput, its power consumption remains a concern.
- 译文: 尽管该设计实现了高吞吐率, 但其功耗仍然是一个问题. 
- 技巧: although/though/while表让步时, 主句常用"但/却"呼应. 

6. **目的状语从句**——译为"为了""以便""从而"

- 原文: The tiling strategy is adopted so that data reuse can be maximized.
- 译文: 采用分块策略, 以便最大化数据复用. 
- 技巧: so that/in order that表目的时, 译为"以便/为了". 

### (三)名词性从句

1. **主语从句**——译为"……的是……""……这一点……"

- 原文: What matters most is the balance between computation and memory access.
- 译文: 最关键的是计算与访存之间的平衡. 
- 技巧: what引导的主语从句常译为"……的(事情/内容)". 

2. **宾语从句**——一般顺译, 用"认为""表明""指出"等动词衔接

- 原文: The results show that the proposed design outperforms the baseline.
- 译文: 结果表明, 所提设计优于基线. 
- 技巧: that引导的宾语从句常可译为独立分句, 用逗号隔开. 

3. **表语从句**——译为"……是……"

- 原文: The key issue is that memory access dominates the execution time.
- 译文: 关键问题在于, 访存占据了主要的执行时间. 
- 技巧: "the key issue is that"译为"关键在于/问题在于". 

4. **同位语从句**——译为"……这一……""即……"

- 原文: The fact that the design achieves 5.26x speedup demonstrates its effectiveness.
- 译文: 该设计实现了5.26倍加速这一事实, 证明了其有效性. 
- 技巧: 同位语从句常译为"……这一+名词", 或拆分为两个分句. 

5. **whether/if引导的从句**——译为"是否"

- 原文: Whether the design can be scaled to larger tiles remains to be verified.
- 译文: 该设计能否扩展到更大的分块仍有待验证. 
- 技巧: whether...or not常译为"是否……". 

### (四)长难句拆分技巧

1. **找主干**——先识别主谓宾, 再处理修饰成分

- 原文: The dataflow architecture that we propose, which is based on a tiling strategy, achieves up to 5.26x throughput improvement compared to the baseline on the Xilinx Zynq-7020 platform.
- 拆分: 主干=The dataflow architecture achieves improvement. 修饰=that we propose / which is based on a tiling strategy / compared to the baseline / on the platform.
- 译文: 我们提出的、基于分块策略的数据流架构, 在Xilinx Zynq-7020平台上相比基线实现了最高5.26倍的吞吐率提升. 

2. **顺译法**——按原文顺序逐段翻译, 适合叙述性长句

- 技巧: 当英文语序与中文接近时, 可顺译, 用逗号或分号断开. 

3. **逆译法**——先译后置成分, 再译主干, 适合定语/状语后置的长句

- 技巧: 英文后置定语、状语较多时, 中文常需前置. 

4. **拆句法**——把长句拆成多个短句, 用"这""该""其"衔接

- 技巧: 中文忌长句, 遇到多个从句嵌套时, 拆成短句更通顺. 

5. **合并法**——把多个短句合并为一个长句, 适合并列结构

- 技巧: 英文用and/or连接的并列成分, 中文可合并为"以及""和"等. 

6. **转换法**——词性转换, 如名词转动词、形容词转副词

- 原文: The improvement of data utilization efficiency is achieved through tiled computation.
- 译文: 通过分块计算, 提升了数据利用效率. 
- 技巧: 英文名词化表达多, 中文宜转为动词. 

7. **增补法**——补充省略成分, 使中文语义完整

- 技巧: 英文常省略主语、连接词, 中文翻译时需补出. 

8. **减词法**——省略冗余的代词、连接词、冠词

- 技巧: 英文的the/a、that、which等在中文中常可省略.
